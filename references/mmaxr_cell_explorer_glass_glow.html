Nucleus

The nucleus contains most of the cell’s DNA and helps coordinate cell activities. The nucleolus inside it is involved in making ribosome components.

Think of it as the cell’s information centre — not a literal “brain”.

Plant vs animal cell

QUICK COMPARISON

🌱 Plant cell

Cell wall, chloroplasts in photosynthetic cells, and usually a large central vacuole. Often appears more box-like.

◉ Animal cell

No cell wall or chloroplasts. Usually has a more flexible outline and smaller vacuoles or vesicles.
